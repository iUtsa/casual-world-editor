Point at a table or a drawing. Hold the phone steady; landscape works best.
——Mac: —
Live mode: this phone checks every frame itself (is it steady? is it sharp? did the scene change?) and only asks the Mac to rebuild the 3D world when something on the table changed and the phone is held still. Add or move an object, hold still for a second, and the Mac's 3D world and any running experiment update.

Frames go only from this phone to the Mac on your Wi-Fi. If the camera doesn't start, use “Photo”.
